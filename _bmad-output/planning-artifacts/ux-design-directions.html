Direction A: Clean & Confident

Recommended

White canvas with warm amber accents. Professional and trustworthy. Photo hero with clear info hierarchy below. Sticky bottom bar with running total and CTA. Most aligned with "booking a hotel room" mental model.

Generous whitespace Warm but restrained Trust-forward Airbnb-like clarity

Direction B: Bold & Immersive

High Impact

Full-bleed hero with text overlay. Equipment photo dominates. Warm amber sticky bar at bottom. More emotional and visual-forward. Great for making equipment feel exciting and premium.

Photo-dominant Immersive Amber-forward Uber-like feel

Direction C: Minimal & Structured

Efficient

Information-dense with clear sections. Everything visible at once. Sectioned cards for calendar and pricing. Most efficient for repeat users. Stripe checkout influence.

Information-first Compact sections Quick decisions Checkout-like

Direction D: Card-Forward

Modular

Everything in cards on a tinted background. Each section is a distinct, elevated card. Gradient total bar. Most app-like feel. Great modularity for complex flows.

Card-based App-like Colorful accents DoorDash-like
